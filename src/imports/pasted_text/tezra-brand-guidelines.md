ROLE & CONTEXT
You are a senior UI/UX designer tasked with designing a complete, production-ready website 
for Tezra Media — a multi-vertical media company operating across PR & Consultation, 
News Publishing, Multimedia, and Arts. The design benchmark is Forbes.com: editorial 
authority, structured content density, and professional gravitas. The result must feel like 
a premium media institution, not a startup.

Typography System:

  navigation, labels, and UI elements.
- Accent / Pullquote Font: "Cormorant Garamond" Italic — used sparingly for 
  pullquotes, art descriptions, and taglines.

Font scale (desktop): 
  Hero Headline: 64–72px | Section Title: 36–42px | Card Title: 22–24px | 
  Body: 16–17px | Caption/Label: 12–13px | Navigation: 14px uppercase tracked

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY & AESTHETIC DIRECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Aesthetic: "Editorial Power" — a refined, magazine-grade design system that 
communicates credibility and multi-industry expertise.

Core principles:
1. Structured content density — think Forbes homepage: grid-based cards, clear 
   hierarchy, category labels, rich editorial imagery.
2. Deep navy dominance — the #010066 navy should own headers, top nav, hero 
   sections, footer, and section accents. White and light grey make up content areas.
3. Gold as the prestige signal — use #C8A951 only for: CTA button borders/hover 
   states, section divider lines, category tag highlights, and decorative rule lines.
4. Restrained whitespace — professional density, not sparse minimalism. 
   Cards are information-rich but visually clean.
5. Sharp editorial grid — 12-column grid, consistent gutters, clear visual rhythm.
6. Zero gradients on backgrounds — flat, confident color blocks only.
7. Photography-forward — every card, hero, and feature section assumes a 
   strong editorial image. Design for vivid imagery, not placeholder boxes.

DO NOT use: purple tones, gradient mesh backgrounds, bubbly border-radius above 6px 
on cards, neon accents, or playful/startup aesthetics. This is a media institution.
