# **Product Requirements Document (PRD): Tezra Media Website Development**

**Line Spacing: 1.25**

## **Project Overview**

The purpose of the Tezra Media website is to establish a modern, professional, and user-friendly digital platform that effectively communicates the company’s services, media presence, and industry insights. The website will be designed to provide visitors with seamless access to information while maintaining a clean and intuitive user experience.  
The platform will also serve as a central hub for media-related news, industry updates, and client engagement, with a strong emphasis on accessibility, branding, and content organization.

## **1\. Objectives**

### **Primary Objectives**

* Develop a clean and modern website interface  
* Ensure easy navigation and accessibility for users  
* Present company services and industry expertise effectively  
* Create clear engagement pathways for potential clients  
* Build a scalable structure that supports future expansion

### **Secondary Objectives**

* Establish Tezra Media as a trusted media and consultancy brand  
* Support regular publication of news and industry updates  
* Improve lead generation through strategic calls-to-action  
* Leverage deep user-behavior analytics to consistently streamline and optimize content delivery

## **2\. Target Audience**

### **Primary Audience**

* Corporate organizations and business partners  
* Financial institutions and treasury professionals  
* Energy, power, and renewable sector stakeholders  
* PR and communication clients  
* Investors, art collectors, and buyers

### **Secondary Audience**

* Media professionals and journalists  
* Industry researchers  
* General website visitors seeking industry news, artistic portfolios, and insights

## **7\. Technical Requirements & Analytics Stack**

### **Frontend**

* Responsive website design  
* SEO-friendly semantic structure  
* Optimized loading performance (lazy loading for high-res art portfolios)

### **Backend / CMS**

* Content Management System (CMS)  
* Easy article publishing and catalog management workflow  
* Admin access for content updates and inventory/order tracking  
* Scalable architecture for future feature expansions

### **Embedded Analytical Tools**

To ensure the business can successfully thrive on data analysis and effectively streamline content toward the right markets, the platform will embed the following tools:

* **Google Analytics 4 (GA4):** For comprehensive tracking of traffic sources, user demographics, page-view duration, and content-interaction metrics across different sections.  
* **Hotjar / Microsoft Clarity:** To collect visual heatmaps and anonymous session recordings, allowing the team to analyze how users navigate the art catalog and where they drop off in the reading or buying pipeline.  
* **Conversion & Pixel Tracking (Meta / LinkedIn Pixels):** To accurately track, measure, and optimize B2B client acquisition campaigns and art-buyer lead generation.

## **8\. Future Expansion Considerations**

The platform architecture should support:

* Additional media, video broadcast, and podcast categories  
* Expanded interactive e-commerce and payment gateway scaling for the Arts section  
* New corporate consulting service offerings  
* Scalable data tracking models to adapt to shifting user interests

## **9\. Success Metrics & Data-Driven KPIs**

### **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

* **Traffic & Readership Growth:** Overall website traffic and average time-on-page for news articles.  
* **Market Streamlining Efficiency:** Conversion rates on targeted content segments (e.g., verifying if power/energy readers engage with related consulting CTAs).  
* **Art Catalog Engagement:** CTR (Click-Through Rate) on portfolio items, catalog downloads, and purchase inquiry completions.  
* **Lead Generation Performance:** Form submissions, direct email clicks, and social media click-through rates.  
* **Technical Benchmarks:** Mobile usability scores and Core Web Vitals (page speed, loading optimization).
